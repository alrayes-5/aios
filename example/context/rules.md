# Rules

> Company setup. Made up, see `example/README.md`.

## The AI may do alone

- Read everything in the workspace except `private/`.
- Write plans, drafts and summaries in `plans/` and `outputs/`.
- Compare the numbers in `data/kpi-tracker.md` with their targets.

## Needs approval first

- Sending anything to a customer, supplier or member: Dana reviews every draft and sends it.
- Anything about prices, hours, or spending: Leo or the Monday meeting decides.
- Changing the recipe list or production quantities: Sam decides.
- Deleting or overwriting a file that was not created in this session.

## Data that never goes in

- Names, phone numbers or addresses of customers or wholesale contacts.
- Member pay, bank details, passwords, supplier contracts.
- Anything a customer asked us to keep private.

Refer to wholesale accounts as "Account A", "Account B" and so on. The key to the letters stays in `private/`.

## If a rule gets in the way

Stop, say which rule, and ask Dana.
