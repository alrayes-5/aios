# Strategy

> Made-up strategy for illustration. See `example/README.md`.

## Current Focus Period

Q4 2026, October to December.

## Strategic Priorities

1. Keep every wholesale delivery on time through the holiday season.
2. Cut bread waste from about 9% to 5% of loaves baked.
3. Sign two new wholesale accounts without adding a second oven shift.

## What Success Looks Like

- On-time deliveries at 98% or better every week.
- Waste at or below 5% by the end of December.
- Two new wholesale accounts signed and delivering by 15 December.

## Key Decisions or Open Questions

- Does a fourth delivery run pay for itself, or does the oven schedule need to change first?
- Baking classes use the oven on Saturdays. Do they stay through December?
