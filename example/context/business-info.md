# Business Info

> Made-up company for illustration. See `example/README.md`.

## Organization Overview

Kestrel Bakery Cooperative is a worker-owned bakery in a mid-sized town. It bakes sourdough and pastries every morning and sells through its own shop and to local cafes and restaurants. Eight people share ownership and decisions.

## Products / Services / Focus Areas

- Retail shop: bread, pastries, coffee, six days a week.
- Wholesale: daily bread delivery to local cafes and restaurants.
- Weekend baking classes for small groups.

## Key Context

- Seven years old, eight members, profitable but thin margins.
- One production oven is the bottleneck: it runs from 3am to 11am.
- Wholesale grew over the last year while the retail shop stayed flat.
- Two members are part-time; everyone does a shift in the shop.
